How to play

Aim with the racket’s UP / DOWN / LEFT / RIGHT controls, or the keyboard arrow keys. Click HIT or press Space to send the ball.
Right edge +15, left −15, top +5, bottom −5. Build the highest score you can.
Every 12 hits, take a Coffee Break. Coffee adds one cup. NO keeps you playing.

0–2: sleepy.
3–4: faster, with stars.
4–6: the sweet spot.
7–9: control slips; the ball drifts toward penalties.
10: LOVE! Ten more hits end the game.

Hit quickly to trigger Stir × Spin.
